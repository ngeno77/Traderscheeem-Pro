Trade Responsibly

At TradersCheem, your well-being matters. Understand the risks and never treat simulated trading as a way to earn guaranteed money.

$

Set a Budget

Never use money needed for essential expenses. Practice with demo features instead.

◷

Take Breaks

Take regular breaks from screens and make time for school, family and other activities.

⚠

Know the Risks

Financial trading involves risk. Past results do not guarantee future results.

♡

Emotional Control

Step away if you feel stressed, upset or pressured. Never make financial decisions because of emotions.

Trading Guidelines

✓ Do

  • Learn about financial risks
  • Keep records of simulated activities
  • Understand how demo accounts work
  • Take regular breaks
  • Ask a trusted adult for guidance

✕ Don't

  • Chase losses with bigger amounts
  • Use borrowed money
  • Believe in guaranteed profits
  • Make decisions under pressure
  • Ignore financial risks

ⓘ Warning Signs

If an activity is causing problems, take a break and speak with someone you trust.

⚠ Spending more time than planned
⚠ Feeling pressured to keep participating
⚠ Hiding activities from others
⚠ Neglecting school or relationships
⚠ Feeling unable to take a break
